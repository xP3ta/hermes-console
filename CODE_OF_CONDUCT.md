# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in Console to be respectful, constructive, and
safe. Be considerate, disagree with ideas rather than people, accept good-faith
feedback, and respect privacy. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, doxxing, or publishing another person's credentials or private data
are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `hola@xpetalab.dev`. Reports will be
reviewed as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, reject contributions, restrict participation, or ban participants
when necessary to protect the community.

Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith is prohibited.
