Generative Icons (chat icons)
=============================

The ten icons in this directory (reasoning, terminal-agent, tool-call,
delegate-task, parallel-tools, response-stream, check, error, queued,
agent-team) come from the Generative Icons library published at
https://generativeicons.com (LottieFiles). The library's catalogue
(https://generativeicons.com/catalog.json, schema version 1, library version
0.1.0) declares `"license": "MIT"` for every icon. The catalogue states no
copyright line; the credit is "Generative Icons, LottieFiles".

What is shipped here is a derivative: each icon (outline style, weight 2,
"light" motion, `a/<id>.json` of the source `.lottie`) was rasterised frame by
frame at 64 px with lottie-web 5.13.0 (MIT, build time only, not shipped) and
packed into one lossless WebP strip, recoloured white so the app tints it at
paint time. `SOURCES.json` records the SHA-256 of every source asset and every
strip; `tool/chat_icons/build_atlas.py` rebuilds the strips from the frames.
