Google Play badge artwork

The "Get it on Google Play" badge images in docs/badges/ (google-play-en.png,
google-play-es.png) are the official, unmodified artwork published by Google LLC
at https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/ . They are used only to link to
the app's Google Play listing, under the Google Play badge guidelines
(https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/), which require the artwork to stay
unmodified and the notice "Google Play and the Google Play logo are trademarks
of Google LLC." to appear where it is used.
